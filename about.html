About Us

What is FCIT Student Activities Portal?

FCIT Student Activities Portal is a simple website for students in the Faculty of Computing and Information Technology at King Abdulaziz University. It shows information about student clubs, workshops, activities and events in one place.

Why We Created This Project

Activity information can be shared in different places, so students may miss a workshop or have difficulty finding its time and location. We created this portal to make FCIT activity information easier to find.

The Problem

Students may spend extra time searching for club announcements, workshops and events. Important information can also be missed when schedules are shared in different places.

The Solution

The portal collects FCIT club information and activity schedules in one simple website. Students can quickly check available clubs, activity dates, times and locations.

Project Benefits

The website helps students save time, find activities more easily and reduce the chance of missing workshops or events.
